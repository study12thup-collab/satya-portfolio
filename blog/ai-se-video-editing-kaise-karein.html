AI Se Video Editing Kaise Karein: Beginners Ke Liye Simple Workflow

Satya · 18 October 2026 · 6 min read

Aaj kal AI video editing ka sabse bada helper ban gaya hai. Jo kaam pehle ghanton mein hota tha, jaise captions, silence hatana ya long video se shorts nikalna, ab minutes mein ho jaata hai. Lekin AI sirf madadgaar hai. Idea, story aur final decision aapka hi hona chahiye. Yeh rahi ek simple workflow.

AI kin kaamon mein madad karta hai

  • Script aur ideas: topic ke outline, hook ke options, title ke suggestions.
  • Captions aur subtitles: bol-chaal ko text mein badalna.
  • Silence aur "umm" hatana: rough cut jaldi taiyar hota hai.
  • Noise remove karna: background shor kam karke awaaz saaf karna.
  • Long video se shorts: lambe video ke best moments alag clip mein.
  • Background hatana, resize karna: ek video ko 9:16, 1:1 aur 16:9 mein badalna.

7 step ki workflow

  1. Idea aur outline: AI se 5 se 10 hook ya outline maangein, phir apne experience aur apni bhasha se badal dein. Seedha copy-paste na karein.
  2. Shoot: acchi roshni aur saaf awaaz sabse zaroori hai. AI kharab awaaz ko poori tarah theek nahi kar sakta.
  3. Rough cut: silence aur galat takes AI se hatayein, phir khud dekhein ki flow theek hai.
  4. Captions: auto captions lagayein, phir poora padh kar sahi karein. Hindi aur Hinglish mein galtiyan aksar aati hain.
  5. Music aur sound: sirf wahi music use karein jiska use karne ka haq aapke paas ho ya platform ki library ka ho.
  6. Export: Reels/Shorts ke liye 9:16 (1080 x 1920), YouTube ke liye 16:9.
  7. Cover/thumbnail: har video ka ek saaf cover rakhein jisme chhota text ho.

Kahan AI par bharosa nahi karna

  • Hook aur story: pehle 3 second kya bolenge, yeh insaan hi acchi tarah decide karta hai.
  • Facts: AI galat jaankari bhi de sakta hai, isliye numbers aur claims khud check karein.
  • Aapka andaaz: har video mein ek jaisi hi AI wali awaaz aur template se channel ki pehchaan kam ho jaati hai.
Ek video, kai content: ek lamba video shoot karke usse 3 se 5 shorts nikaalein. Yeh kam mehnat mein zyada posts ka sabse aasan tareeka hai.

Common galtiyan

  • Auto captions ko bina padhe lagana.
  • Har cheez AI par chhod dena aur khud ka angle na daalna.
  • Zyada effects aur transitions, jisse video ka point kho jaata hai.
  • Copyright wala music.

Video editing ka kaam hume dein
