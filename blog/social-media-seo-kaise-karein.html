Social Media SEO Kaise Karein: Instagram Aur YouTube Par Search Se Customer Laayein

Satya · 18 October 2026 · 6 min read

Pehle log har cheez Google par dhoondte the. Ab bahut se log tutorial, reviews aur recommendations ke liye seedha Instagram, YouTube aur dusre social apps mein search karte hain. Isliye aapki posts ko "searchable" banana zaroori ho gaya hai. Ise social media SEO kehte hain.

1. Pehle jaanein log kya search karte hain

Instagram ya YouTube ke search bar mein apna topic type karein, aur neeche jo suggestions aate hain wahi log search karte hain. Jaise "logo design" type karne par aane wale options. Google ke autocomplete se bhi yeh dekh sakte hain. Is list se 10 se 15 keywords likh lein.

2. Caption ki pehli line mein keyword

Caption ki shuruaat mein saaf likhein ki post kis baare mein hai. Jaise: "YouTube thumbnail kaise banayein: 3 aasan steps". Sirf emoji ya "Wait for it" jaise caption se platform ko samajh nahi aata.

3. Video mein bolein aur likhein

Video mein jo aap bolte hain aur jo text screen par dikhta hai, dono se platform aur dekhne wale samajhte hain ki video kis baare mein hai. Isliye topic ka naam shuru mein bolein aur screen par bhi likhein, aur captions lagayein.

4. Profile ko bhi searchable banayein

  • Naam: "Satya Official" ke saath ek keyword, jaise "Satya Official | Designer".
  • Bio: aap kya karte hain aur kis shehar mein, ek line mein.
  • Link: website ka link zaroor rakhein.

5. Hashtags kam aur sahi

3 se 5 relevant hashtags kaafi hain. Sau hashtags daalne se kuch nahi hota. Topic se milte-julte, aur kuch chhote niche wale hashtags chunein.

6. Series aur playlist

YouTube par videos ko playlist mein rakhein aur Instagram par highlights ya series banayein. Isse log ek ke baad ek aapke videos dekhte hain, aur channel ki pehchaan bhi banti hai.

7. Comments ka jawab dein

Sawaalon ke jawab dene se baatcheet badhti hai, aur comments mein aane wale sawaal naye content ke ideas bhi dete hain.

Dhyan rakhein: keywords ko jabardasti thoosna (keyword stuffing) ulta asar karta hai. Natural bhasha mein likhein, jaise aap kisi insaan ko samjha rahe ho.

Ek chhota udaharan

Maan lijiye aap logo designer hain. Reel ka caption: "Business ke liye logo kaise chunein: 3 galtiyan jo log karte hain". Video mein pehle hi bolein "Business logo chunte waqt yeh 3 galtiyan mat karna", screen par bhi yahi likhein, aur bio mein likhein "Logo & brand designer, Azamgarh". Ab jab koi "logo kaise banwayein" search karega, to aapka content milne ke chance badh jaate hain.
