Business Ke Liye Logo Kaise Chunein: Sahi Logo Ka Checklist

Satya · 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

Logo aapke business ka chehra hai. Customer sabse pehle ise hi dekhta hai, board par, Instagram profile par, bill par aur packaging par. Is checklist se aap bina confusion ke sahi logo chun sakte hain.

1. Pehle business ko samjhein

Designer ko logo banane ko kehne se pehle yeh likh lein: aap kya bechte hain, customer kaun hai, aur aap kaisa feel dena chahte hain (premium, friendly, modern, traditional). Jitna clear brief, utna kam revision.

2. Simple rakhein

Acha logo simple hota hai. Agar bachcha bhi use ek baar dekhkar yaad rakh sake to wo kaam kar raha hai. Bahut saari details, gradients aur chhote text chhote size par kho jaate hain.

3. Chhote size par test karein

Logo ko Instagram profile photo (bahut chhota circle) mein, visiting card par aur phone ke icon ke size mein dekhein. Agar tab bhi saaf dikhta hai, to sahi hai.

4. Black and white mein bhi chalna chahiye

Kai baar logo ek rang mein chhapta hai, stamp mein ya bill par. Isliye ek ranga version bhi hona chahiye. Agar sirf rangon ke bharose chal raha hai, to wo kamzor logo hai.

5. Colors aur font sochkar chunein

  • 1 main color aur 1 support color kaafi hain.
  • Food aur local business ke liye warm colors (laal, orange, peela) aksar acche lagte hain; tech aur finance ke liye neela ya hara.
  • Font padhne mein aasan ho. Ek hi font family rakhein, 3 se 4 alag fonts mat milayein.

6. Copy ya template se bachein

Internet par mile-julte logo ya free templates ka use karne se aapka logo doosron jaisa dikhega, aur kai baar copyright ka issue bhi aa sakta hai. Original ya kam se kam customized design lein.

7. Trademark ka basic check karein

Naam aur logo final karne se pehle India ke trademark registry (ipindiaservices.gov.in) par public search karke dekh lein ki milta-julta naam ya logo pehle se registered to nahi hai. Badhe business ke liye trademark registration karwana sahi rehta hai.

8. Sahi files maangein

Final delivery mein ye files zaroor lein:

  • PNG (transparent background) website aur social media ke liye
  • SVG ya PDF (vector) print, flex banner aur board ke liye
  • Dark aur light background ke liye alag versions
  • Rangon ke codes aur font ka naam
Yaad rakhein: logo sirf drawing nahi, ek pehchaan hai. Ek baar final karke use har jagah same tarah se use karein: website, Instagram, packaging aur bill par.
