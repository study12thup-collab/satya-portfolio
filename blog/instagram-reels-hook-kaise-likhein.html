Instagram Reels Ke Liye Hook Kaise Likhein: 8 Formulas Examples Ke Saath

Satya · 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

Reel mein sabse zaroori hota hai pehla hissa, yaani hook. Agar log pehle 2 se 3 second mein ruke nahi, to baaki video koi nahi dekhta. Acchi baat yeh hai ki hook likhna seekha ja sakta hai. Yeh 8 formulas aap aaj hi use kar sakte hain.

8 hook formulas

  1. Problem hook: "Aapki Reels par views kyun nahi aate?"
  2. Result hook: "Maine ek hafte mein yeh badlav karke apna reach badhaya." (sirf tab, jab yeh sach ho)
  3. Galti hook: "Thumbnail banate waqt zyada tar log yeh galti karte hain."
  4. Curiosity hook: "Is ek setting ne mera kaam aasan kar diya."
  5. Number hook: "Logo banwane se pehle yeh 3 cheezein check karein."
  6. Myth hook: "Log kehte hain business ke liye bada budget chahiye. Aisa nahi hai."
  7. Before/after hook: pehle purana design, phir naya, bina kuch bole.
  8. Seedha sawaal ya pukaar: "Agar aap dukaan chalate hain, to yeh Reel aapke liye hai."

Hook ko asardaar banane ke tips

  • Pehle shabd se shuru karein. "Hello dosto, aaj main bataunga..." jaise lambe intro se bachein.
  • Screen par text rakhein. Bahut log bina awaaz ke dekhte hain, isliye hook text mein bhi dikhe.
  • Visual bhi hook ho. Pehle frame mein kuch dilchasp dikhe: result, product ya koi action.
  • Vaada nibhayein. Hook jo vaada kare, video usi ko poora kare. Bina matlab ke clickbait se log chhod dete hain.
  • Chhota rakhein. Ek line, ek idea.

Reel ka simple structure

  1. Hook (0 se 3 second)
  2. Ek-do line mein problem ya context
  3. Main baat ya demo
  4. Ek saaf CTA: "Save kar lein", "Comment mein batayein" ya "Link bio mein hai"
Test karte rahein: ek hi baat ko 3 alag hooks se bana kar dekhein ki kaun sa zyada ruk kar dekha jaata hai. Instagram Insights mein dekhein ki log kitni der tak dekh rahe hain, phir jo hook chale usi tarah ke aur banayein.

Series bana kar dekhein

Ek hi topic par "Part 1, Part 2, Part 3" jaisi series logon ko wapas laati hai. Har part ke end mein agle part ka hint dein.
