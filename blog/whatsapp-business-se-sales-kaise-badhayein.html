WhatsApp Business Se Sales Kaise Badhayein: Catalog, Labels Aur Quick Replies

Satya · 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Bharat mein zyada tar customer pehle WhatsApp par hi sawal puchte hain. Agar aap abhi bhi normal WhatsApp par kaam chala rahe ho, to WhatsApp Business app (free) par aana sabse aasan upgrade hai. Festive season mein to yeh aur zaroori ho jaata hai.

Yeh 5 features pehle lagayein

  • Business profile: naam, address, timing, website aur description bharein.
  • Catalog: products ya services ki photo, naam aur price daalein, taaki bar bar photo na bhejni pade.
  • Quick replies: baar baar aane wale sawal, jaise price, delivery, payment, ka ek shortcut banayein.
  • Labels: customer ko "New", "Payment pending", "Delivered" jaise labels dein.
  • Away aur greeting message: raat ko ya busy hone par automatic jawab.

Sales badhane ke tareeke

  1. Instagram bio, Google profile aur website par WhatsApp ka direct link daalein.
  2. Har inquiry ka 10 minute ke andar jawab dene ki koshish karein.
  3. Order confirm hone par payment, delivery date aur tracking saaf bhejein.
  4. Delivery ke baad feedback maangein, aur khush customer se review ya reference.
  5. Status par naye products aur offers daalein.
Sirf ijazat wale ko bhejein: broadcast ya promotional message sirf unhi ko bhejein jinhone aapse baat ki ho ya message lene ki ijazat di ho. Anjaan numbers ko bulk message bhejna spam hai aur WhatsApp aapka number block kar sakta hai.

Common galtiyan

  • Har baar lamba paragraph bhejna. Chhote, saaf message rakhein.
  • Bina poochhe group mein add karna.
  • Price aur delivery ki baat pehle saaf na karna.
