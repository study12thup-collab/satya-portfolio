Google Business Profile Se Local Customer Kaise Payein (Azamgarh Jaise Shehar Ke Liye)

Satya · 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Jab koi "mere paas logo designer" ya "Azamgarh mein digital marketing" search karta hai, Google map ke saath kuch business upar dikhata hai. Yeh jagah Google Business Profile se milti hai, aur yeh bilkul free hai. Chhote shehar mein competition kam hota hai, isliye yahan jaldi nateeja milta hai.

Profile ko poora bharein

  • Business ka sahi naam: wahi naam jo board aur website par hai. Naam mein keywords na thoosein.
  • Category: sabse sahi main category chunein, jaise "Marketing agency" ya "Graphic designer".
  • Address aur timing: sahi aur updated rakhein.
  • Phone aur website: website ka link zaroor daalein.
  • Description: 2 se 3 line mein batayein aap kya karte hain aur kis shehar mein.

Photos aur posts

  • Logo, cover photo, dukaan ya office ki asli photos daalein.
  • Apne kaam ke samples daalein.
  • Hafte mein ek chhota update ya offer post karein.

Reviews kaise maangein

Khush customer ko seedha review ka link bhejein, aur bas itna kahein: "Aapka feedback hamare liye bahut madadgaar hoga." Kabhi paise ya discount dekar review na khareedein, aur nakli review na likhwayein. Google aisi profile ko hata sakta hai. Har review ka shukriya ke saath jawab dein, achhe ka bhi aur bure ka bhi.

Yaad rakhein: verify karna zaroori hai. Bina verification ke profile search mein theek se nahi dikhti. Verification ka tareeka Google khud batata hai (video, call ya postcard).

Aaj ka checklist

  1. Profile claim aur verify karein.
  2. Sab details aur category sahi karein.
  3. 5 asli photos daalein.
  4. Website ka link jodein.
  5. 3 purane customers se review maangein.

Apna business Google par laana hai?

Profile setup se website tak, Satya aapke business ko online dikhne mein madad karta hai.
