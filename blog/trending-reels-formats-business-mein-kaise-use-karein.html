Trending Reels Formats Ko Apne Business Mein Kaise Use Karein (Bina Cringe Hue)

Satya · 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Har hafte Instagram par koi na koi naya format viral hota hai. Problem yeh hai ki log use bina soche copy kar lete hain, aur video cringe lagta hai. Trick yeh hai ki trend ka format lein, par baat apne customer ki karein.

Abhi chal rahe kuch formats (Oct 2026)

  • Nickname hook: "Mujhe sab ___ bulate hain" se shuru karke apna kaam bataana.
  • Ek sawal, ek shabd: sawal puchna aur jawab sirf ek shabd mein dena.
  • Pain point vs solution: ek side customer ki problem, doosri side aapka solution.
  • "That's so you" collage: photos ka collage jisme customer khud ko dekhe.

Yeh examples Under30CEO ke October 2026 ke trends article se hain. Trend jaldi badalte hain, isliye apne Reels feed mein bhi dekhte rahein.

4 step mein use kaise karein

  1. Trend dekhein aur save karein: jo format aapko pasand aaye use save kar lein.
  2. Customer ki problem se jodein: apne customer ka sabse bada sawal ya dikkat kya hai, wahi video ki baat banao.
  3. Do version shoot karein: ek trend ke jaise, ek apne style mein.
  4. 48 ghante baad check karein: Insights mein dekhein kaun sa version zyada dekha gaya aur save/share hua.

Dhyan rakhne wali baatein

  • Trend 1 se 2 hafte mein hi post karein, uske baad woh purana lagta hai.
  • Apni awaaz aur bhasha rakhein, kisi aur ki nakal na karein.
  • Business account par trending gaane ka license alag ho sakta hai, isliye platform ki library ya licensed music hi lein.
  • Har trend aap par fit nahi hota. Jo aapke customer se na jude, use chhod dein.
Yaad rakhein: trend sirf dhakka deta hai. Video tabhi kaam karta hai jab usme customer ke liye kuch kaam ki baat ho.

Source: Under30CEO (Instagram trends, October 2026).
