Diwali 2026 Marketing: Social Media Content Plan Aur Sale Ideas (Chhote Business Ke Liye)

Satya · 26 October 2026 · 6 min read

Is saal Diwali 8 November 2026 ko hai, yaani ab bahut kam din bache hain. Festive season mein log gift, decoration, sweets aur kapde sab kharidte hain, isliye chhote business ke liye yeh sabse accha mauka hota hai. Bas plan pehle se hona chahiye, warna last din ki bhaag-daud mein kaam kharab hota hai.

Sabse pehle 3 cheezein tay karein

  • Offer: kya discount ya combo dena hai, jo aapko nuksaan na de.
  • Delivery cutoff: kis tareekh tak ka order Diwali se pehle pahunch jayega. Yeh date sabko saaf batayein.
  • Stock: festive items ka stock pehle se check kar lein, warna order lekar mana karna padta hai.

4 stage ka content plan

  1. Teaser (10 se 14 din pehle): "jaldi aa raha hai" type ke posts, collection ki jhalak, countdown stories.
  2. Launch (5 se 7 din pehle): offer ka poora announcement, gift guide, combo packs.
  3. Festival days: greeting reel, last-minute order reminder, delivery cutoff yaad dilana.
  4. Diwali ke baad: thank you post, customer feedback, agle offer ka hint.

Sale ideas jo chhote business par chalte hain

  • Combo pack: 2 ya 3 cheezein saath mein, ek gift box jaisa.
  • Early-bird offer: pehle 20 ya 30 orders par chhota extra faayda.
  • Referral: purane customer dost ko bataye to dono ko benefit.
  • Corporate gifting: dukaan, office aur company ke liye bulk gift options.
  • Service business ke liye: festive creatives pack, jaise posts, reels aur banner ek saath, tay price par.

Content ideas

  • Packing ka behind-the-scenes reel.
  • "Is Diwali kya gift dein" ka gift guide.
  • Apni team ya apni awaaz mein Diwali greeting reel.
  • Asli customer ka feedback ya photo (unki ijazat se).
  • Stories mein countdown aur sawal-jawab (delivery, payment, return).
Imaandaar promotion: discount asli hona chahiye. Pehle daam badhakar phir "50% off" dikhana galat hai, aur bhramak vigyapan par Consumer Protection Act 2019 ke tahat karyavahi ho sakti hai. WhatsApp par sirf unhi ko message bhejein jinhone aapse baat karne ki ijazat di ho. Business account mein reels ke liye platform ki music library se hi gaane lein.

Aaj ka 24 ghante ka action list

  1. Offer, delivery cutoff aur stock tay karein.
  2. Teaser ke 3 posts aur 2 stories banayein.
  3. Profile bio mein offer aur order link daalein.
  4. Pichle customers ko apna offer batane ki list banayein.

Sources: Brut India, AnySocialMedia (Diwali 2026 ki tareekh aur festive marketing ke general tareeke).
