UGC Ads Kya Hote Hain Aur Business Ke Liye Kaise Kaam Karte Hain

Satya · 18 October 2026 · 6 min read

UGC ka matlab hai User Generated Content. UGC ads wo ads hain jo ek asli insaan ke andaaz mein bante hain, jaise koi customer ya creator apne phone se product use karke apna experience bata raha ho. Studio wale chamakdaar ads ke muqable ye zyada natural lagte hain, isliye log inhe jaldi skip nahi karte.

UGC ad aur normal ad mein fark

  • Normal ad: scripted, polished, brand ki awaaz mein.
  • UGC ad: phone se shoot, bol-chaal ki bhasha, real insaan ki awaaz mein.

Social media par log doston aur creators ki baat par zyada bharosa karte hain, isliye UGC style Instagram Reels aur Meta (Facebook) ads mein popular hai.

Ek acchi UGC ad ka structure

  1. Hook (pehle 3 second): aisi line jo ruk kar dekhne par majboor kare. Jaise "Mere dukaan ki sales kyun nahi badh rahi thi?"
  2. Problem: wo takleef jo customer ko hai.
  3. Solution/demo: product ko use karke dikhayein, sirf bolein nahi.
  4. Proof: result, before/after ya asli feedback.
  5. CTA: saaf bolein ki ab kya karna hai ("Link par click karke order karein").

Shoot karte waqt dhyan dein

  • Vertical video (9:16), phone ka behtar camera aur acchi roshni.
  • Awaaz saaf ho. Shor-sharabe wali jagah se bachein.
  • Subtitles zaroor lagayein, kyunki bahut log video bina awaaz ke dekhte hain.
  • 15 se 30 second ka video aksar kaafi hota hai.

Creator ya customer ko brief kaise dein

Poora script ratne ko mat kahein. Ek chhota brief dein: product kya hai, 2 se 3 main fayde, kya bolna zaroori hai aur kya nahi. Creator ko apne shabdon mein bolne dein, wahi UGC ki asli taakat hai.

AI kahan madad karta hai

AI tools script ke ideas, hook ke options, captions, subtitles aur editing ko tez kar sakte hain. Lekin product use karte hue asli insaan ka experience aur asli feedback ki jagah koi nahi le sakta.

Dhyan rakhein: jhoote testimonial ya nakli reviews kabhi use na karein. Meta aur dusre platforms ke ads ke rules ke khilaaf hai aur ad account band ho sakta hai. Hamesha asli customer ya creator ka content use karein.

Common galtiyan

  • Pehle 3 second mein hi brand ka logo aur lamba intro daal dena.
  • Ek hi ad chalana. 3 se 5 alag hooks test karein aur jo chale use badhayein.
  • Seedha bechne ki koshish. Pehle problem aur fayda dikhayein.
  • Subtitles aur CTA bhool jaana.
